## Steps

### Step 1: Plunger cup

Start with sphere 40mm x 40mm x 40mm.

The center of the sphere should be on the workplane (hence the -20.00mm in the diagram)

<LinkedImage src={plunger1} alt="Plunger cup"/>

Duplicate and turn red 

<LinkedImage src={plunger2} alt="Plunger cup"/>

With this red sphere, we need to shrink it to 36mm x 36mm x 36mm without moving the object.

You achieve this by clicking the right lower corner white square, pressing **Alt** and **Shift** 
while also clicking and shrinking that white square until it read **18mm**. 

<LinkedImage src={plunger3} alt="Plunger cup"/>

You now see the original blue sphere because it is bigger. You can see the red one inside by selecting
the blue sphere and  pressing **t** for transparent on your keyboard. Press **t** again to make solid.

<Image src={plunger4} alt="Plunger cup"/>

Now drag a torus

<Image src={plunger5} alt="Plunger cup"/>

Now select both and align

<Image src={plunger6} alt="Plunger cup"/>

Select both shapes and Union group 

<Image src={plunger8} alt="Plunger cup"/>

drag a box and make sure it completely covers the shapes above the workplane.

<Image src={plunger9} alt="Plunger cup"/>

<Image src={plunger10} alt="Plunger cup"/>


Press Ctrl+A to select all then union group
