## Key dimensions


#### Outer Dimensions (The "Box")

* Length: 15.8mm
* Width: 31.8mm
* Height: 9.6mm (This is the height of the brick body, excluding the studs/pips).

------------------------------
#### Inner Cavity (The "Hole")

* Width: 13.4mm (Explicitly labeled in the center view).
* Length: 29.4mm (Calculated: 31.8mm total - 1.2mm wall thickness on each side).
* Height: 8.6mm (Calculated: 9.6mm total - 1.0mm top thickness).

------------------------------
#### Studs / Pips (Top Cylinders)

* Diameter: 4.8mm
* Height: 1.8mm
* Spacing: 8.0mm (Center-to-center).

------------------------------
#### Inner Tubes (Bottom Cylinders)

Center Bottom Tube


* Diameter: 5.51mm
* Radius: 2.755mm
* Wall Thickness: 0.6mm 

Outer Bottom Tubes

* Diameter: 4mm
* Radius: 2mm
* Wall Thickness: 0.6mm

### Step 5: Position pip accurately

<LinkedImage src={lego6} alt="Lego brick step 6" />

This is tricky. 

1. Drag a ruler onto lego brick in top left corner.
2. Click on the three dashes on the ruler and select **Use midpoint**
3. To get a perfect top down view, click on top on view cube and switch to flat view.
4. The dimensions in green specify the distance from ruler origin to midpoint of pip.

### Step 6: Create pip array or pattern

<LinkedImage src={lego7} alt="Lego brick step 7" />

Before starting, make sure grid unit is 1.0mm

1. Select pip
2. Select duplicate or **Ctrl+D**
3. Hit **right arrow** 8 times to go **8.0mm** to right
4. Hit **Ctrl+D** 2 more times to complete row

### Step 7: Create both rows

<LinkedImage src={lego8} alt="Lego brick step 8" />

1. Select all pips (select one, then **Shift** select the others)
2. Hit **Ctrl+D**
3. We need to move down **7.98mm** . We will achieve this in two Steps
   - move down **8.0mm**
   - put the ruler on the bottom left corner and use the green dimension to adjust offset
